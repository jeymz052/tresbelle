## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intersection of medical precision and high-end editorial wellness. Tailored for a discerning clientele seeking bespoke dermatological and aesthetic treatments, the visual personality is serene, indulgent, understated, and impeccably curated. 

The aesthetic philosophy draws heavily from **Warm Minimalism** and **Contemporary Editorial Luxury**. Layouts favor generous, breathing negative space, deliberate structural hierarchy, and tactile softness over hyper-digital gloss. Visual rhythm is quiet rather than loud, prioritizing clinical credibility paired with the sensory comfort of an elite spa sanctuary. Tactile details rely on subtle tonal stepping, micro-borders with low opacity, and graceful type contrasts between literary serifs and neutral modern sans-serifs.

## Colors

The palette balances therapeutic warmth against refined clinical contrast:

- **Primary (`#8B5B43`)**: A grounded, rich espresso-taupe evoking organic earth, leather, and premium architecture. Used for primary CTAs, active states, key interactive indicators, and anchoring structural elements.
- **Secondary (`#C89B7B`)**: A luminous, brushed-sand terracotta tone used for secondary accents, treatment badges, subtle hover fills, and decorative iconography.
- **Tertiary (`#A85353`)**: A restrained dusty rose-brick used judiciously for featured medical procedures, seasonal packages, delicate notifications, and soft alerts.
- **Neutral (`#2B2625`)**: A deep, warm charcoal that replaces harsh pure black, providing high-contrast readability without breaking the serene tone.
- **Named Surfaces**:
  - `surface-cream` (`#FDFBF9`): The primary atmospheric canvas background, imparting an airy, natural linen undertone.
  - `surface-blush` (`#FDF1F0`): A delicate petal tint utilized for card containers, treatment summary wrappers, and tender callouts.
  - `accent-gold` (`#D4AF37`): Reserved for VIP memberships, premium aesthetic tiers, and delicate micro-embellishments.
  - `border-subtle` (`#EBE3DC`): A pale oat-gray line tone for hair-thin architectural separations.

## Typography

The typographical pairing expresses the dual nature of an elite medical spa: clinical clarity paired with bespoke high-fashion luxury.

- **Editorial Serifs (`Playfair Display`)**: Applied to all top-level titles, quote highlights, and primary banners. Its high stroke contrast and sculptural terminals reference haute couture journals and classical dermatology texts.
- **Modern Geometric Sans (`DM Sans`)**: Chosen for its clean, geometric balance and exceptional legibility across complex medical intake forms, treatment descriptions, dosage guidelines, and patient booking flows.
- **Letter Spacing & Casing**: Uppercase styling is strictly reserved for `label-sm` category pills, protocol tags, and subtle metadata to maintain an architectural rhythm. Large headlines must always remain in natural sentence or title case with tight optical kerning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces an airy, museum-grade spacing model based on an 8-point baseline grid. 

- **Grid Architecture**: 
  - **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 48px (`3rem`) outer canvas margins. Treatment showcases utilize 6-column splits or asymmetrical 4/8 compositions.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 32px margins.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) margins.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: White space serves as a primary luxury material. Sections are separated by sweeping vertical buffers (`space-xl` scaled to 4rem - 6rem on marketing views) to evoke stillness and eliminate digital claustrophobia.

## Elevation & Depth

In alignment with clean medical elegance, this design system avoids heavy drop shadows, plastic skeuomorphism, and dark ambient occlusion. 

Visual hierarchy relies on **Tonal Layering** and **Ghost Outlines**:
- **Base Surface**: Crisp `surface-cream` creates the foundation.
- **Elevated Surfaces**: Modals, hovering treatment cards, and booking step drawers use pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with an ethereal, tinted ambient shadow: `0 12px 36px -8px rgba(43, 38, 37, 0.06)`.
- **Card Framing**: Elements rely primarily on 1px borders colored in `border-subtle` (`#EBE3DC`) rather than deep shadows.
- **Floating Overlays & Sticky Navbars**: Finished with 80% opacity and a `12px` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), subtly diffusing underlying imagery while establishing quiet elevation.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft** shape language (`roundedness: 1`), honoring the architectural balance of classic aesthetics and contemporary clinical precision.

- **Interactive Elements & Inputs**: Finished with a standard `4px` (`0.25rem`) border radius, communicating structured clinical authority and bespoke restraint.
- **Cards & Media Panels**: Utilize `8px` (`0.5rem`) corners (`rounded-lg`), softening patient consultation photography and before-and-after galleries.
- **Chips & Treatment Badges**: May deploy pill radii (`9999px`) only when indicating fleeting procedural tags, status indicators, or active doctor filter toggles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#8B5B43` background with white label text, `4px` radius, and 14px uppercase/medium tracking (`label-lg`). Hover shifts softly to `#724935` with a smooth 200ms ease transition.
- **Secondary / Outline**: 1px border in `#8B5B43` over transparent canvas; hover fills with a delicate `#8B5B43` at 6% opacity.
- **Tertiary / Text**: Raw text button accompanied by an inline directional arrow, underlined with an intentional 1px offset line in `#C89B7B`.

### Cards & Treatment Containers
- **Service Cards**: Rendered with `#FDFBF9` background, 1px border in `#EBE3DC`, and `rounded-lg` (8px). Visual media within cards features a 4:5 editorial aspect ratio with image zoom on hover (1.02x scale).
- **Pricing & Package Tiers**: Highlighted using `surface-blush` backgrounds with a subtle vertical gold accent bar (`#D4AF37`) along the left edge.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Filter Chips**: Height of 32px, `rounded-full`, with `#EBE3DC` 1px border and `#2B2625` body text. Selected state inverts cleanly to a `#8B5B43` fill with white text.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- **Text & Date Fields**: Clean, flat inputs with a 1px border in `#EBE3DC` that transitions to `#8B5B43` on focus. Background is solid `#FFFFFF`. Floating labels use `label-sm` uppercase styling in `#8B5B43`. No bulky drop shadows.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Radio Selectors**: 18px circular indicators with a refined 1.5px border. Active selection fills with a 10px centered dot in `#8B5B43`.
- **Checkboxes**: 18px square with `2px` roundedness; active state triggers a crisp checkmark icon centered on `#8B5B43`.

### Medical Spa Specific Components
- **Before / After Comparison Sliders**: Clean split-image interaction framed in a thin 1px border, utilizing a slim taupe hairline handle with an understated circular divider (`#8B5B43`).
- **Doctor / Specialist Credentials Plaque**: Clean profile cards embedding accreditation badges, clinic affiliations, and clinical approach summaries framed by delicate double-lined rules.
